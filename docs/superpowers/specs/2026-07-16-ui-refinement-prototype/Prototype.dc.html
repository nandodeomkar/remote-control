WORKING PROTOTYPE
Synced to the Flutter source. Things to try:
  • Tap the status chip to cycle Connected → Reconnecting → Disconnected (disconnected greys out the hub tiles)
  • Every tool screen has a floating Trackpad bubble — drag it to an edge, tap to pop the trackpad window; ⤢ opens full screen
  • Trackpad: drag to move, tap to click, long-press to lock dragging, tap to drop
  • Keyboard: tap a modifier to arm, long-press to lock; switch Nav / Fn / Num
  • Power: slide a track to the end — destructive ones open the red countdown
  • Presenter: Start show runs the timer; tap it to pause, hold to reset
  • The gear opens Settings — sensitivity, scroll, haptics, the locked Blue accent
