<objective>
Build the four full-screen keyboard pads (Type, Arrows, Numpad, Fn) and the bottom tab bar as reusable, service-agnostic widgets consuming the public `KeyCap` and the `capsForPad`/`KeyboardPad` primitives from 10-03. No screen wiring yet — the pads take injected callbacks exactly like `KeyRows`, so their widget tests need no service fake.

Output: `lib/keyboard/key_pads.dart` (4 pads + `KeyboardTabBar` + the moved `kKeyboardHint`) and per-pad widget tests.
</objective>
