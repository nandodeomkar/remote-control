<objective>
Add the trailing star button to every tab row: tri-state rendering from `TabEl.bookmarked`, a
nested tap recognizer that must not activate the tab, an instant optimistic flip on star, a confirm
gate on unstar, and a state-announcing semantic label.

Purpose: D-09 exists because the tab row's gesture budget is already spent — tap-down activates,
long-press closes, and horizontal drag closes — so a star has to be a trailing button. D-11 is the
truthfulness rule: the star must never misreport, which is why UNKNOWN renders nothing at all and
why removing is confirm-gated rather than fire-on-tap.

Output: `kStarSemantic`, `kUnstarSemantic`, the star slot threaded through the tab-section widget
chain, the `_onStarTab` handler, and the widget tests that pin the arena behaviour.
</objective>
