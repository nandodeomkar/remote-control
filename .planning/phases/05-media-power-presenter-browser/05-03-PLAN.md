## Phase Goal

**As a** couch-bound laptop owner, **I want to** control music and volume,
safely lock/sleep/restart/shut down the PC, drive slide shows, and steer the
browser from my phone, **so that** the everyday control-center extras work
end-to-end without leaving the couch.

_Slice framing:_ Step 2 of the volume slice — the wire contract (05-01) now
becomes observable behavior: any authed session receives the volume snapshot
and live changes, testable end-to-end in CI with a fake client and a
test-driven watch channel. 05-05 plugs the real Windows producer into this
seam; 05-07 renders it.

Purpose: the push plumbing (SC1 "stays accurate when volume is changed on the
PC itself") with the security gate (no pre-auth leak — RESEARCH Pitfall 8)
proven in CI.
Output: VolumeState type, ServerDeps.volume seam, session branch + snapshot,
integration tests, "1.1" server version.
