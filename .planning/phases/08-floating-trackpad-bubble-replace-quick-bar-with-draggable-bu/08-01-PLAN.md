<objective>
Create the two independent, non-secret position stores + Riverpod notifiers that the bubble and window persist to. No REQ-IDs are formally mapped to Phase 8 (ROADMAP shows TBD); this plan backs the UI-01 quick-bar → bubble replacement by persisting its two positions. Implements decisions D8-02 (bubble `(edge, yFraction)`, default right/70%) and the persistence half of D8-03 (window `(x, y)` fractions, unset sentinel).

Purpose: These are leaf modules with zero dependencies — landing them (and their tests) first unblocks the bubble (08-03) and window (08-04) in Wave 2. They clone the established `SensitivityStore`/`ScrollDirectionStore` two-half house pattern exactly, so risk is low and behavior is a copy of proven code.

Output: `lib/settings/bubble_position.dart`, `lib/settings/window_position.dart`, and their round-trip/degrade unit tests.
</objective>
