## Phase Goal

**As a** person controlling my PC from my phone, **I want to** see and manage my open windows and apps and move copied text between the two devices, **so that** I can switch apps, launch favorites, and share clipboard text without touching the laptop.

<objective>
Close Phase 6 with a real-device gate. The apps thread (input injection / foreground-lock), real window enumeration + icons, arboard clipboard interop, live pushes, Android 10+ resume-sync, and sensitive-clip skip are NOT CI-testable — this plan runs the full automated suite as the regression floor, then walks the owner through the on-device UAT checklist that proves SC1–SC5.

Purpose: The manual verifications listed in 06-VALIDATION.md "Manual-Only Verifications" require a real phone + PC and cannot be automated. This gate is where they are confirmed before verification.

Output: A green full-suite run + a signed-off on-device UAT checklist. No production code changes.
</objective>

  <what-built>Real WinApps (window enumeration + icons + focus/close/launch) and WinClipboard (arboard get/set + live push + sensitive/echo filtering) on the PC, plus the Apps and Clipboard screens with favorites, history, and opt-in auto-sync on the phone.   <how-to-verify>
    Run the server on the Windows PC and the app on the Android phone (paired, same LAN), then verify each success criterion (checklist from 06-VALIDATION.md "Manual-Only Verifications"):

    1. APPS-01 (SC1) — Open the Apps screen: the "Open windows" list shows your real windows in Alt-Tab order with app icons and NO ghost/blank/cloaked entries. Tap a row → that window comes to the foreground (or the taskbar button flashes — best-effort is acceptable). Tap close on a row → the window closes; close a window on the PC first, then tap its stale row → a "That window is no longer open." toast appears.
    2. APPS-02 (SC2) — Long-press a running CLASSIC app (browser/editor/terminal) → "Pinned {app}" and it appears in Favorites. Close that app, then tap the favorite → it relaunches. Long-press a Store/UWP window → "This app can't be pinned yet." (non-pinnable, no pin).
    3. APPS-03 (SC2) — With the Apps screen open, open and close apps on the PC → the window list updates within ~1s via `apps.changed`, no manual refresh.
    4. CLIP-01/02 (SC3) — Copy text on the phone → tap "Send to PC" → paste on the PC shows it ("Sent to PC."). Copy text on the PC → tap "Fetch from PC" → paste on the phone shows it ("Copied to phone."). CLIP-04 (SC5): both appear in "Recent"; tap a history entry → it re-pushes to the PC.
    5. CLIP-03 (SC4) — Turn Auto-sync ON. Copy on the PC → it appears on the phone live. Copy on the phone while the app is BACKGROUNDED → nothing syncs; bring the app to the foreground → the phone copy reaches the PC (resume-sync; the honesty note is visible). Copy once with auto-sync on both ways → the value does NOT bounce back (no loop). Copy from a password manager (KeePassXC / Windows Credential Manager) with auto-sync ON → the secret does NOT sync to the phone (sensitive skip).
    6. Wire-size guard — Open many windows (aim for 20–40) → the Apps list still updates and the session stays connected (icons 32×32, list capped ≤50, under the 64 KB frame cap).

    Report any failures with the criterion number; failures are captured as gaps for `/gsd-plan-phase --gaps`.
  </how-to-verify>
