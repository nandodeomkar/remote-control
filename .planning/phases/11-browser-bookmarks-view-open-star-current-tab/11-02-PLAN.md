<objective>
Build the Rust bookmark relay tier: core-owned bookmark data types, the `Bookmarks` executor
trait and its extension-backed implementation, the `bm.state` ingress arm on the loopback ext
listener, and the composition-root wiring.

This plan deliberately does NOT touch the phone-facing codec (`codec/json.rs`) or the router —
those land in 11-03. It stops exactly at the boundary where the tree still compiles and the whole
existing suite stays green.

Purpose: D-06 requires the whole tree in one pushed snapshot, which means a second watch channel
alongside `tabs`. Pitfall 2 is the failure this plan exists to prevent: `run_ext` resets
`tabs_tx` at three independent sites, and a missed one leaves the phone rendering a stale,
tappable bookmark tree next to a correct "install the extension" hint.

Output: `BmNode`, `BookmarksState`, `trait Bookmarks`, `Executors.bookmarks`,
`TabInfo.bookmarked`, `ExtCmd::BmOpen/BmStar/BmUnstar/BmRemove`, `BookmarksRelay`, the
`"bm.state"` arm, `reset_relay_watches`, main.rs wiring, and six new `ext_relay.rs` tests.
</objective>
