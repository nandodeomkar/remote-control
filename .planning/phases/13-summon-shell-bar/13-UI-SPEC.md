# Phase 13 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for the **summon shell**: the permanent two-icon bottom bar (`SummonBar`), the raised **empty** surface (`SummonSurface`), its inert 48dp handle, and the motion that connects them. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.
>
> **The design system is LOCKED and inherited. Nothing here is a redesign.** The seven palette consts in `app/remote_control/lib/theme/tokens.dart` are the only colours on the phone (`test/theme_tokens_guard_test.dart` **fails the build** on any other hex literal under `lib/`); `Symbols.<name>_rounded` from `material_symbols_icons` **4.2951.0** is the only icon family (`test/icons_guard_test.dart` bans classic `Icons.*`, allowlist **empty**). Spacing, typography and motion vocabulary carry forward from the approved 07/09/10/11/12-UI-SPECs verbatim.
>
> **This phase is a host restructuring, not a feature.** Its visible output is deliberately close to nothing: a 56dp bar with two glyphs on it, and a `kSurface` slab that slides up behind the bar and contains a handle and empty space. **That emptiness is the deliverable** (D-13-08) — every pixel specified below is chrome that Phases 14 and 15 inherit unchanged.
>
> **Upstream decisions are the spine.** `13-CONTEXT.md` D-13-01…D-13-19 and `13-RESEARCH.md` §6–§9 are binding. This document is prescriptive within the two areas CONTEXT left open — *bar and surface visual composition inside the existing tokens*, and *the mid-animation reversal / curve vocabulary* — and it resolves **three** conflicts between locked decisions and locked success criteria in § Conflict Resolutions. The executor implements these values; it does not re-derive them.

## Typography

**This phase renders zero glyphs of text.** The bar is icon-only (D-13-12: *"Icons carry all the visual weight"*), and D-13-08 forbids a placeholder label inside the overlay. The table below is the inherited app-wide role set, recorded so the checker's Typography dimension has the contract it validates against and so a later reader can see the omission was decided, not forgotten.

| Role | Size | Weight | Line Height | Phase-13 usage |
|------|------|--------|-------------|----------------|
| Heading | 16px | 600 | 1.2 | **not used** |
| Group heading | 14px | 600 | 1.3 | **not used** |
| Row title | 16px | 400 | 1.5 | **not used** |
| Body | 14px | 400 | 1.5 | **not used** |
| Label | 12px | 400 | 1.3 | **not used** |

**Binding notes:**

- Weights available app-wide are exactly `w400` and `w600`. Phase 13 uses neither because it renders no text.
- **Do not add a label under the bar icons.** `KeyboardTabBar` pairs a 24px glyph with a 12px label in the same 56dp box, and that is the correct shape for a *four-way page selector* whose destinations are not otherwise nameable. The summon bar has two icons whose meanings are already taught by the hub tiles that carry the same two glyphs (`hub_screen.dart:89, :97`), and D-13-12 asks for deliberate emptiness. Labels would also fight Phase 17's "you are here" treatment for the same 56dp of height. The names ship as **tooltips/semantics** instead (§ Copywriting Contract).
- **Do not add a title, hint, or height readout inside the overlay** (D-13-08). Phase 13's overlay is empty on purpose: a placeholder string is exactly the thing that "can confuse a failure" that the phase ordering exists to prevent.

| Role | Value | Token | Usage |
|------|-------|-------|-------|
| Dominant (60%) | `#0E0F12` | `kBg` | The bar's fill (D-13-12) — the bar reads as the edge of the screen, not a component on it. Also everything behind, which is the host screen, untouched |
| Secondary (30%) | `#16181D` | `kSurface` | The raised surface's fill (D-13-08) — the one thing in the phase that is a distinct plane |
| Accent (10%) | `#4C8DFF` | `kAccent` | see reserved-for list |
| Destructive | `#FF6B6B` | `kError` | **NOT USED IN THIS PHASE.** See § No Destructive, Error, or Empty Copy |

**Fixed non-accent:** `#2A2D34` `kHairline` (the bar's 1px top rule, the surface's 1px top rule — the phase's only two hairlines), `#E6E8EB` `kText` (a bar glyph at rest while connected), `#6B7280` `kMuted` (a bar glyph while disconnected or disabled; the drag-handle glyph, always).

**Accent reserved for (explicit, nothing else):**

- **The bar glyph of the surface that is currently raised** (D-13-11). That is the entire accent budget of this phase — one 24px glyph, at most one at a time, only while a surface is up.

**Accent is never** the bar's fill, the bar's hairline, the surface's fill, the surface's hairline, the handle glyph, a border, a ripple colour, or a connection signal. There is **no resting accent anywhere in this phase**: with nothing raised, the entire summon chrome is `kBg` + `kHairline` + two `kText`/`kMuted` glyphs. That absence is the signal, exactly as it is on the Updates screen (12-UI-SPEC § Focal point).

**Accent budget check.** A permanent bar on all fourteen screens is the highest-frequency chrome in the app. Anything accent-tinted at rest would put a persistent blue focal point on every screen in the product, competing with `ConnectionHeaderChip`'s connected state, the keyboard's selected pad tab, the drag-lock pill, and the Presenter's armed chip — all of which already own accent for genuinely transient states. One accent glyph, present only while a surface is raised, is the smallest treatment that makes the toggle legible, which is precisely what D-13-11 asked for.

## Conflict Resolutions

Three places where a locked decision and a locked success criterion cannot both be satisfied literally. Each is resolved below in favour of the **stated purpose** of the decision, with the reason recorded so an auditor does not read it as drift and a later reader does not "restore" the literal wording.

### CR-1 — The bar's resting glyph tint is `kText`, not `kMuted`

**The tension.** D-13-11 says the non-raised icon *"stays `kMuted`"*. D-13-13 says *"**when disconnected**, both icons render `kMuted`"* — a clause that is vacuous unless the connected resting state is something else. They cannot both be literal.

**Resolution.** Resting-and-connected is **`kText`**; disconnected is **`kMuted`**; raised is **`kAccent`**.

**Why this serves both decisions.** D-13-11's stated purpose is *"what makes the tap-to-dismiss toggle legible rather than a guess"* — `kAccent` (#4C8DFF) against `kText` (#E6E8EB) is a full hue separation plus a large luminance step, so the toggle is at least as legible as accent-against-muted, and arguably more so. D-13-13's operative content — *stay tappable when disconnected*, and *never subscribe to `ConnectionService.changes`* — is honoured verbatim. The literal word `kMuted` in D-13-11 is read as **"never accent"**, which is what the sentence is for.

### CR-2 — BAR-03's disabled state is a **glyph swap**, not a tint change

**The tension.** ROADMAP SC4 requires the keyboard icon to be *"**visibly** disabled while the PIN field holds focus"* — and names the two screens where that must be observable: **the pairing sheet and the device list**. Both are, by definition, screens the user is on **because the PC is not connected**. Under CR-1 the icon is already `kMuted` there. A tint-only disable would therefore be invisible on exactly the two screens SC4 names.

**Resolution.** The disabled keyboard icon renders a **different glyph** — `Symbols.keyboard_off_rounded` — in `kMuted`, in the identical 56×56 slot at the identical 24px size, with `onPressed: null` and no ripple.

**Why.** The seven-token palette offers exactly three usable glyph tints on `kBg` (`kText`, `kMuted`, `kAccent`; `kError` is banned for non-destructive state by house rule, `kHairline`/`kSurface` are invisible at glyph weight). Four states need distinguishing. A glyph swap is the only palette-legal fourth channel, it costs nothing, it uses the already-pinned icon family, it reads at a glance, and it survives colour-blindness and a screen reader — which a tint never would. It also fixes the sentence a non-visual user hears (§ Copywriting Contract).

**Geometry is untouched by the swap.** `keyboard_off_rounded` occupies the same 24×24 box. Assert that the bar's `Rect` and both icon slots' `Rect`s are byte-identical across the swap (13-RESEARCH §8: *"Disabled ≠ hidden"*).

### CR-3 — The 48dp handle band costs the raised trackpad 49dp of content

**The tension.** D-13-07 ships the 48dp handle as inert chrome in Phase 13 so *"the geometry this phase pins is the geometry that ships"*. D-13-14 sets the trackpad surface's starting height at ~280dp. `280 − 1 (hairline) − 48 (handle band) = 231dp` of actual pad — against the **234dp** of pad in the `FloatingTrackpadWindow` being retired (13-RESEARCH §6.4).

**Resolution.** Ship **280** as the starting value (D-13-14 is locked and the tuning session is Phase 13's UAT), and **carry the 231-vs-234 number into the UAT script as a named observation**, not as a footnote. It is a one-constant change if the owner judges it short.

**Why this matters beyond Phase 13.** CONTEXT already warns *"Do not let Phase 14 assume RET-03 passes."* This is the arithmetic behind that warning made explicit: at the starting constant the raised trackpad is **3dp smaller** than the affordance it replaces. Phase 14's RET-03 verdict is downstream of a number tuned here. Record the tuned value in the Phase 13 UAT record so Phase 14 inherits a fact, not an assumption.

## Geometry

All measurements are logical pixels (dp). The arithmetic that produces `available` is **owned by 13-RESEARCH §6.1 and is not restated here** — this section specifies only what is *drawn* inside the boxes that arithmetic yields.

### Named constants introduced by this phase

```dart
const double kSummonBarHeight        = 56;   // D-13-13 (LOCKED) — content only, excl. the safe inset
const double kSummonHairline         = 1;    // both top rules — bar and surface
const double kSummonIconSlot         = 56;   // one bar icon's tap box, square
const double kSummonIconGap          = 32;   // the single gap between the two slots
const double kSummonGlyphSize        = 24;   // both bar glyphs AND the handle glyph
const double kSummonHandleBandHeight = 48;   // D-13-07 / SUM-05 — reserved in 13, driven in 14

// DESIGN CHOICES (D-13-14), not derived numbers. Name them as such in code so a
// later reader does not treat them as arithmetic. Both are clamped to `available`
// by the ONE shared derivation — never a per-surface formula.
const double kSummonTrackpadHeight   = 280;  // "standard Android IME height"
const double kSummonKeyboardHeight   = 432;  // D-13-14's "~430", rounded onto the 4dp grid
```

**Why 432 and not 430.** 430 is not a multiple of 4 and would be the phase's only off-grid number. 432 is, it is inside D-13-14's *"~430dp starting value"*, and it buys 2dp of the very thin headroom documented below.

### The bar

```
Positioned(left: 0, right: 0, bottom: barBottomOffset, height: kSummonBarHeight)
└─ DecoratedBox(color: kBg, border: Border(top: BorderSide(kHairline, width: 1)))
   └─ Row(mainAxisAlignment: MainAxisAlignment.center, children: [
        SummonIconButton(trackpad)   // 56 × 56
        SizedBox(width: 32)
        SummonIconButton(keyboard)   // 56 × 56
      ])
```

- **Total icon pair width = 56 + 32 + 56 = 144dp**, centred. On the narrowest plausible Android viewport (320dp) that leaves 88dp dead on each side; on the 411dp reference device, 133.5dp. **The dead width is left visibly dead** (D-13-12) — no third slot, no spacer content, no `Spacer()` that a future icon could be dropped into.
- **The pair is centred, not distributed.** `MainAxisAlignment.center`, never `spaceEvenly`/`spaceAround`. Both icons land in one thumb's arc, and the composition survives a hypothetical third icon (v2+) without moving the existing two to new absolute positions.
- **Each slot is 56×56**, which is `kSummonBarHeight` square: it exceeds the 48dp touch floor on both axes and it makes the slot's rect trivially derivable in a test (`barRect.height`).
- **The top border is 1px `kHairline`, full bar width**, drawn *inside* the 56dp (a `Border` on a `DecoratedBox` does not add height). It never becomes 2px and it never becomes accent — that treatment belongs to `KeyboardTabBar`'s selected tab and must not be echoed here or Phase 17 will have nothing left to say.
- **`bottom: barBottomOffset`** where `barBottomOffset = max(viewPadding.bottom, viewInsets.bottom)` (13-RESEARCH §6.1). The gesture-nav inset is padded **below** the 56dp, so the glyphs never sit in the system gesture zone where the trackpad icon would compete with swipe-up-to-home (D-13-13).
- **The bar is the LAST child of the shell `Stack`** — it paints over the sliding surface (which is what makes the surface appear to emerge from behind it, with **no `ClipRect` and no `saveLayer`**) and it hit-tests first (`Stack` hit-tests in reverse paint order), which is BAR-02's *"stays visible and tappable the whole time"*. 13-RESEARCH §7.4.

### The bar icon button — `SummonIconButton`

One widget, two instances, one 4-value state function.

```
SizedBox(56, 56)
└─ InkWell(onTap: <null when disabled>, child: Center(
     child: Icon(<glyph>, size: 24, color: <tint>)))
```

| Priority | Condition | Glyph | Tint | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | This surface is currently raised | base glyph | `kAccent` | tap → lower (SUM-03) |
| **2** | Keyboard icon **and** a local text field holds focus (BAR-03) | `Symbols.keyboard_off_rounded` | `kMuted` | `onPressed: null`, **no ripple** |
| **3** | Connection is not `ChipStatus.connected` | base glyph | `kMuted` | tap → raise (**still works** — D-13-13) |
| **4** | Otherwise (resting, connected) | base glyph | `kText` | tap → raise (SUM-01) |

Base glyphs — both already const in the tree at `hub_screen.dart:89, :97`:

| Icon | Glyph |
|------|-------|
| Trackpad | `Symbols.touch_app_rounded` |
| Keyboard | `Symbols.keyboard_rounded` |

**Binding state rules:**

1. **Priority order is strict and defensive.** State 1 beats state 2: once Phase 15 lands, a raised keyboard focuses the shell's *own* hidden IME node, which D-13-17's predicate explicitly excludes — so the pair is unreachable, and the ordering exists only so an unexpected focus can never disable the affordance that dismisses the thing holding focus. State 1 beats state 3: raised-while-disconnected is `kAccent`, because the tint describes the **overlay**, not the link — the raised surface shows its own disconnected state (D-13-13).
2. **The trackpad icon never enters state 2.** BAR-03 gates the *keyboard* icon only. `Symbols.touch_app_rounded` has no `_off` variant in this contract and none is to be added.
3. **Layout never shifts between any two states.** Same slot, same glyph size, same gap, same bar rect. Only the glyph identity, the tint, and the presence of a tap handler change. Pin this with a `Rect` equality assertion across all four states, not with an eyeball.
4. **No ripple on a disabled icon.** `onTap: null` on the `InkWell` removes it for free — the `_HubTile` offline idiom verbatim (`hub_screen.dart:235`).
5. **No press flash, no scale, no haptic on the bar.** The anti-feature list bans haptics on summon/dismiss; the `InkWell` ripple is the entire press feedback, matching `KeyboardTabBar` (`key_pads.dart:359`).
6. **The bar reads connection through `connectionStatusProvider` (value-equal) and nothing else** — **never** a `ConnectionService.changes` subscription and never a `Timer.periodic` (D-13-13; 13-RESEARCH P9). The bar is mounted on the trackpad's hot path on every screen; a per-packet rebuild here is a cursor-latency regression, not a cosmetic one.

### The raised surface — `SummonSurface`

```
Positioned(left: 0, right: 0,
           bottom: barBottomOffset + kSummonBarHeight,
           height: <animated height>)
└─ Transform.translate(offset: Offset(0, (1 - curvedRaise) * height))
   └─ MediaQuery.removePadding(removeBottom: true,          ← MANDATORY, §6.3
        child: DecoratedBox(
          color: kSurface,
          border: Border(top: BorderSide(kHairline, width: 1)),
          child: Column(children: [
            SummonHandle(),                                  ← 48dp band, full width
            Expanded(child: <EMPTY in Phase 13>),            ← Phases 14 / 15 fill this
          ]),
        ))
```

**Binding geometry rules:**

1. **The surface's bottom edge rests on the bar's top edge.** `surfaceRect.bottom == barRect.top`, always, including every frame of a raise, a lower and a swap. This is what SC2's *"the surface sits on top of it"* means — **vertical stacking, not z-order** (13-RESEARCH OQ-3). Pin it as invariant **I1**, per device, computed from driven metrics.
2. **The surface's height is `summonSurfaceHeight(s, available)`** from the **one shared derivation** (D-13-06), clamped to `available`. Never a per-surface formula, never a screen fraction (D-13-14 superseded the fractions), never a per-pad clamp (D-13-15).
3. **`MediaQuery.removePadding(removeBottom: true)` around the overlay is mandatory** and must be probe-asserted in Phase 13 even though nothing inside consumes padding yet: `MediaQuery.paddingOf(handleContext).bottom == 0` while the driven `viewPadding.bottom == 48`. Without it, the surface silently grows by 24–48dp of dead space the moment Phase 15 mounts `KeyboardTabBar` and its inner `SafeArea` (13-RESEARCH §6.3, failure mode F5).
4. **Square top edge. No corner radius, no `ClipRRect`, no shadow, no elevation.** Reasons, so nobody "improves" it later: (a) a radius on a slab that is flush to both screen edges and resting on the bar reads as a broken card, not a sheet; (b) honouring a radius requires clipping the child, and a per-frame clip on the surface that will hold the trackpad's hot path is exactly the `saveLayer` cost P9 names as one of only two real jank sources here; (c) the in-tree precedent for this exact component — the docked keyboard panel's `_kPanelHairline` — is a square 1px rule; (d) a shadow is a per-frame blur on a half-screen animating surface for no information gain. The `FloatingTrackpadWindow`'s 16px radius + shadow is correct **for a floating card** and is being retired; do not port it.
5. **The surface is a `Positioned` **sibling** of the Navigator, never an ancestor.** Nothing beneath it is re-laid-out, resized, reflowed or scrolled (SUM-02), and a tappable widget outside the surface still receives its tap. This is not an IME-style resize contract (`ROADMAP § Milestone-Wide Constraints`).
6. **Do not animate with `Opacity` / `AnimatedOpacity` / `AnimatedPositioned`.** `Transform.translate` only — the first two force a `saveLayer` per frame, the third re-lays-out per frame (13-RESEARCH § Anti-Patterns).

### The handle — `SummonHandle` (inert in Phase 13, driven in Phase 14)

```
SizedBox(height: 48, width: double.infinity)     ← the reserved BAND
└─ Center
   └─ SizedBox(48, 48)                            ← the TAP TARGET (Phase 14 attaches here)
      └─ Icon(Symbols.drag_handle_rounded, size: 24, color: kMuted)
```

- **48dp band, 48×48 tap target, 24px glyph, `kMuted`, horizontally centred.** 48 is SUM-05's literal number and the app's touch-target floor; the tap target is a real 48×48 box (not an icon-sized hit region) so an accessibility service has something to drive in Phase 14.
- **`Symbols.drag_handle_rounded`** — verified present in the pinned `material_symbols_icons` 4.2951.0. Not `drag_indicator_rounded` (that is the `FloatingTrackpadWindow`'s *move* affordance, a ⠿ six-dot grip, which means "drag me around the screen" — the wrong verb for a dismiss handle) and not a hand-drawn pill `Container` (a new off-grid measurement for a glyph that already exists).
- **The handle glyph is always `kMuted`.** It has no enabled/disabled/raised/connected variation, in this phase or in Phase 14. It is an affordance, not a state.
- **In Phase 13 the handle has no gesture recognizer at all** (D-13-07: *inert chrome*). Do **not** add a no-op `GestureDetector` "for later" — an affordance that swallows a tap and does nothing is worse than one that visibly does nothing.
- **Phase 14 must attach its `Listener`/`GestureDetector` to the tap target as a SIBLING of the surface content, never as an ancestor of it** — the `floating_trackpad_window.dart:245-257` rule verbatim. Stated here because the band is being reserved now and the trap is invisible until the trackpad is inside it.

### The content box Phases 14 and 15 inherit

This is the single arithmetic Phase 13 owes downstream. Declare it as a named function beside the height constants.

```
surfaceContentHeight(s) = summonSurfaceHeight(s, available)
                        - kSummonHairline          //  1
                        - kSummonHandleBandHeight  // 48
```

At the starting constants, on the reference device (`available = 763`, IME down):

|---|---|---|---|

| Keyboard | 432 | **383** | Chip row 64 + tab bar 56 = 120 chrome ⇒ **263** of pad viewport. The Numpad crushed to `KeyCap.minHeight: 48` needs ~248 (13-CONTEXT D-13-14). **~15dp of headroom** — 432 is near the floor, not near the ceiling |

## Motion Budgets

| Element | Duration | Curve | Source |
|---------|----------|-------|--------|
| **Summon (raise)** | **200ms** | `Curves.easeOut` | MOT-01 (locked). Value matches the incumbent `kBubbleExpandDuration` (`bubble_scaffold.dart:50`) — reuse the number, declare a **new** const `kSummonRaiseDuration` in the shell |
| **Dismiss (lower)** | **150ms** | `Curves.easeIn` (as `reverseCurve`) | MOT-01 (locked). `kSummonLowerDuration`, expressed as the raise controller's `reverseDuration` — one controller, two budgets |
| **Swap (D-13-10)** | **200ms** | `Curves.easeOut` on the height lerp | `kSummonSwapDuration`. **One motion**, not a 150 lower followed by a 200 raise |
| Bar icon tint change | **0ms — instant** | none | A state colour that fades is a state you cannot read mid-transition (12-UI-SPEC § Motion Budgets, verbatim house rule) |
| Bar icon glyph swap (CR-2) | **0ms — instant** | none | No `AnimatedSwitcher`, no cross-fade |
| Bar appear / disappear (BAR-04 suppression) | **0ms — instant** | none | The QR screen's bar is absent, not animated out. It is a route-scoped structural fact |
| Handle | **none** | — | Inert in Phase 13 |

**Binding motion rules:**

1. **Enter decelerates, exit accelerates.** `Curves.easeOut` in / `Curves.easeIn` out is the standard read and it is already the house vocabulary (`bubble_scaffold.dart:208` uses `Curves.easeIn` for the collapsing element; `key_pads.dart` uses the `Curves` defaults). **No new curve type** — not `easeOutCubic`, not `fastOutSlowIn`, not a custom `Cubic`. Claude's-discretion call, recorded so it does not get re-litigated.
2. **A tap landing mid-animation reverses from the live value** (`AnimationController.reverse()` / `.forward()`), with **no queue and no input lockout** — CONTEXT § Claude's Discretion, and the framework default. A 150ms reverse from `value == 0.5` settles in ~75ms because the duration is scaled by the remaining distance. Pin that with one test.
3. **`pumpAndSettle` is BANNED for every timing assertion** in the new summon suites — it advances until no frames are scheduled, so a 200ms summon and a 900ms one are indistinguishable. Use the exact `pump(Duration)` sequences in 13-RESEARCH §7.6. `pumpAndSettle` for *navigation* (not timing) is fine.
4. **The swap never detaches from the bar.** During a swap, `surfaceRect.bottom == barRect.top` on **every** frame — that assertion is what proves it is one motion rather than a lower-then-raise (13-RESEARCH §7.6 swap test).
5. **The swap is a dismiss for the outgoing surface.** Phase 13 has no surfaces, so what it owes is a **single named seam** — one `void _onSurfaceDismissed(SummonSurface outgoing)` called from **both** the lower path and the swap path, with a doc comment saying exactly that. If Phase 13 ships two separate call sites, Phase 15 will wire `dismissed()` to one of them and every swap will leave a modifier physically held on the PC (D-13-10).
6. **No new animation primitives.** No shimmer, no skeleton, no scrim fade, no bounce, no spring, no scale, no rotation, no cross-fade between the two surfaces' content.

**Binding interaction rules:**

1. **No swipe-down-to-dismiss, no scrim, no hide-on-scroll, no auto-dismiss** on inactivity, disconnection or screen change. Locked anti-feature list.
2. **No haptic on summon or dismiss.** Locked. The bar is the highest-frequency affordance in the app and a tick on every raise would be constant. (`hapticTick()` remains correct for the *sends* other screens make — this phase sends nothing.)
3. **The bar renders OVER modal routes** — the pairing sheet, the sensitivity sheet, the power confirm dialog. This is an accepted consequence of an app-scope host, **and SC4 requires it** (the keyboard icon must be visibly disabled *on the pairing sheet*, which is only possible if the bar is there). Add *"the power confirm dialog with the bar over it"* to the phase's UAT eyeball list (13-RESEARCH §9). **Do not** "fix" it with a second suppressor.
4. **Modal routes are deliberately un-guarded.** With a dialog or sheet open it is the topmost route, so back closes it first and the raised surface is untouched. Six such sites exist. State it as intended so nobody wires guards into them.
5. **An orphaned surface is dismissible only by the icon tap** (and, from Phase 14, the handle). D-13-03: back from the hub with an orphaned surface raised **exits the app with the surface still raised**. Deliberate, owner-stated, not a bug. Do not add ownership-transfer machinery.

## Copywriting Contract

House rule: **no inline copy literals** — every string below ships as a named `const String` in the file that renders it (the `kRevokeConfirm*` / `kInstallHint*` convention).

| Element | Copy |
|---------|------|
| Primary CTA | **none — the two bar icons are the only affordances and they carry no visible text** (§ Typography) |
| Empty state heading | **none, by decision** — see § No Destructive, Error, or Empty Copy |
| Empty state body | **none, by decision** — see § No Destructive, Error, or Empty Copy |
| Error state | **none — this phase has no failure mode that produces copy** |
| Destructive confirmation | **none — this phase has no destructive action** |

### Tooltips and semantics (the phase's entire string inventory)

| Const | Copy | Attached to |
|-------|------|-------------|
| `kSummonTrackpadLabel` | `Trackpad` | The trackpad bar icon's `tooltip` (Flutter surfaces `tooltip` as the button's semantics label) |
| `kSummonKeyboardLabel` | `Keyboard` | The keyboard bar icon's `tooltip`, in states 1, 3 and 4 |
| `kSummonKeyboardBlockedLabel` | `Keyboard — finish typing on the phone first` | The keyboard bar icon's `tooltip` in state 2 (BAR-03 disabled) |
| `kSummonHandleLabel` | `Dismiss` | `Semantics(label:)` on the handle's 48×48 target. **Phase 13 ships the label with an inert target**; Phase 14 wires the action to it (SUM-05) |

**Copy rules:**

- **The two nouns are byte-identical to the hub tile labels** `Trackpad` and `Keyboard` (`hub_screen.dart:88, :96`). Same word, same glyph, same destination — a second vocabulary for the same two things is the single most confusing outcome available to this phase, and Phase 17 is about to make these two icons and those two tiles talk about each other.
- **The nouns never become verbs and never change with state.** Not `Show trackpad` / `Hide trackpad`. Raised-ness is announced structurally via `Semantics(selected: true)` on the raised icon, not by rewriting the label — because Phase 17 adds a *second* selected-ish concept ("you are here" for the screen you are on) to these same two icons, and a string vocabulary would have to multiply to cover the cross product. One noun, two orthogonal semantic flags.
- **`kSummonKeyboardBlockedLabel` names the problem and the next step** and is the one place this phase spends words, because a `kMuted` slashed glyph is legible but not self-explaining, and a non-visual user gets nothing from either. Precedent: `kSettingsWithUpdateTooltip = 'Settings — update available'` (12-UI-SPEC).
- **No jargon, no blame, no mechanism.** Not `focus`, not `IME`, not `PIN`, not `disabled`, not `you must`. `finish typing on the phone first` says what is true and what to do.
- The separator is an em dash with spaces (` — `), the house form.
- **No toasts, no snackbars, no banners** are introduced by this phase. The only banner in the shell is the **rehomed** `UipiBanner` slot, whose copy is unchanged and whose only production mount moves into it in Phase 16 (SAFE-03).

### No Destructive, Error, or Empty Copy

Recorded as a decision, not an omission, because the checker's Copywriting dimension asks for all three.

- **No destructive action exists in this phase.** Nothing is deleted, revoked, powered off, or made irreversible. `kError` appears nowhere. The house escalation rule (`bookmark_confirm.dart`) reserves confirmation for irreversible machine state and for data removal; raising and lowering an empty overlay is neither, and adding a gate here would train the owner to click through gates where they matter.
- **No error copy exists** because no operation in this phase can fail. There is no IO, no wire traffic, no persistence, no permission. A disconnected PC is **state**, not failure, and it renders as `kMuted` — muted, never red, and never hidden.
- **The overlay's emptiness carries no copy, deliberately** (D-13-08). *"The empty overlay contains nothing throwaway… no placeholder label, no debug height readout. Every pixel of Phase 13's overlay is chrome that Phases 14 and 15 inherit."* An empty-state sentence would be the only string in the phase that ships to be deleted, and it is precisely the kind of throwaway artefact that "can confuse a failure" during the geometry UAT this phase exists to run.

**Do not add** a confirm dialog, a toast, a snackbar, an empty-state illustration, a "coming soon" label, a height readout, or a `kError` element anywhere in this phase.

## Non-Goals (do not render)

- **No third bar icon, no bar labels, no bar overflow menu, no reorderable icons, no swipe-on-bar to switch surfaces** — v2+, per the locked defer list. The dead width stays dead.
- **No scrim, no dim, no blur behind the raised surface.** Nothing beneath it changes appearance at all.
- **No corner radius, no shadow, no elevation, no `ClipRRect`** on either the bar or the surface (§ Geometry rule 4).

- **No gesture on the surface body and no gesture on the handle in Phase 13.** The handle is rendered and inert; Phase 14 drives it.
- **No connection banner, chip, or status text on the bar.** Every connected-capable screen already carries `ConnectionHeaderChip` and `ReconnectingBannerSlot`; the bar adding a third channel for the same fact is redundant chrome on all fourteen screens.
- **No "you are here" / selected-for-current-screen treatment.** That is RET-02 in **Phase 17**, and it must *compose* with D-13-11's raised-tint rather than conflict with it (D-13-11 note). Phase 13 ships only the raised state.

- **No bubble deletion.** Phase 13 ships the bar and the bubble **side by side**; a temporary double affordance is the accepted cost of sequencing Phase 16 after Phase 14 so the owner never loses fast trackpad access.
- **No `AndroidManifest.xml` edit and no `pageTransitionsTheme` edit** (D-13-01). Predictive back is **measured**, not enabled.
- **No new palette literal in `lib/`** (`theme_tokens_guard_test`), **no classic `Icons.*`** and **no `Symbols.get()`** (`icons_guard_test`), **no new off-grid measurement**, **no `pubspec.yaml` edit**.
