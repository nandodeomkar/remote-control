<objective>
Make the host visible: the permanent two-icon bar, the correctly-sized empty surface it
raises, and the one shared derivation that sizes them.

Purpose: the two things that are expensive to change later — the host shape and the
inset arithmetic — are entirely independent of what the overlay contains, and both are
invisibly wrong until a real device with gesture nav and a raised IME. This plan pins
the arithmetic and ships every pixel of chrome that Phases 14 and 15 inherit unchanged.
Output: `summon_geometry.dart`, `summon_bar.dart`, `summon_icon_button.dart`,
`summon_surface.dart`, `summon_handle.dart`, the shell wiring that renders them, the
shared test harness, and the first end-to-end raise/lower suite.
</objective>
