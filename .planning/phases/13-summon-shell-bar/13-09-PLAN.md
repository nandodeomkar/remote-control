<objective>
Gate the keyboard icon on local field focus, and suppress the bar entirely on the QR
scanner.

Purpose: BAR-03 is this phase's one genuine security control. The pairing PIN is a
credential; if the summon keyboard steals focus from the phone's PIN field, the PIN is
typed into whatever window has focus on the laptop and disclosed to every application
there. Milestone research proposed a predicate that is essentially never true in a live
app, which would have shipped a permanently disabled button — the corrected predicate
lands here. BAR-04 removes the bar from the one screen where a raised surface could
obscure credential capture.
Output: the focus gate in the bar, the depth-counted suppressor and its single mount, and
the two suites that pin them.
</objective>
