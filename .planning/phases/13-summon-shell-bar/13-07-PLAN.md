<objective>
Close the silent half of SUM-04: back does not dismiss on any route that was never
guarded, and nothing warns.

Purpose: the existing central wrapper covers the nine tool screens only. Five further
page pushes exist across four files — Settings, Device List, Updates, the QR scanner and
the bubble's own expand — and on each of them back would silently keep popping the route
with a surface still raised. There is no error and no log; the requirement just quietly
does not hold there. This plan routes every push through one seam and adds the guard that
makes a future un-guarded push fail the build.
Output: `pushGuarded<T>`, seven converted call sites, and `test/push_sites_guard_test.dart`.
</objective>
